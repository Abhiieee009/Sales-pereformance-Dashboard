# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze business performance across countries, product categories, and sales channels.

The dashboard helps users understand revenue trends, monitor profitability, compare regional sales performance, and identify the product categories and sales channels contributing to business results.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Track key sales and profitability metrics in one place.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across different countries.
- Understand how different product categories contribute to gross profit.
- Evaluate revenue distribution across sales channels.
- Enable interactive analysis using filters for month, quarter, country, and product category.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures and business calculations
- **Data Visualization** — KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, pie charts, donut charts, and map visualizations

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays key business metrics:

- **Total Revenue** — Overall sales revenue.
- **Total Gross Profit** — Profit generated after accounting for the applicable cost of goods sold.
- **Gross Margin** — Gross profit expressed as a percentage of revenue.
- **Total Quantity** — Total quantity of products sold.
- **Total Orders** — Total number of orders recorded.

### 2. Sales Trend Analysis

- Tracked monthly changes in revenue and gross profit.
- Compared revenue and profitability trends over time.
- Helped identify periods of stronger or weaker sales performance.

### 3. Country-Wise Sales Analysis

- Compared revenue across countries.
- Identified markets contributing to overall sales.
- Used bar charts and map visualizations to explore geographical sales distribution.

### 4. Product Category Analysis

- Examined gross profit contributions across product categories.
- Compared category-level performance to understand which products contributed more to profitability.

### 5. Sales Channel Analysis

- Analyzed revenue distribution across different sales channels.
- Compared channel contributions to understand how sales were generated.

### 6. Interactive Filtering

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Month
- Quarter
- Country
- Product Category

These filters allow users to explore specific periods, markets, and product segments.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard supports business decision-making by helping users:

- Monitor sales and profitability in one place.
- Identify high-performing countries and product categories.
- Understand monthly changes in business performance.
- Compare sales channels and their contribution to revenue.
- Explore business data interactively to support planning and performance reviews.
